Know About Me

My name is Rajarajeshwari S, and I come from Melmatti Vinnamangalam, a small village in Tiruvannamalai district. I was raised in a close-knit family of four members. Although we live a simple life in a small home, we have always valued happiness, unity, and hard work. I am proud to be the first graduate in my family.

I completed my 10th standard with 78% and my 12th standard with 72%, achievements that I hold with great pride. Due to financial constraints, I was unable to immediately continue my higher education. However, instead of giving up, I looked for alternative paths to build my career.

That turning point came when I learned about Freshworks STS Academy. Joining the program marked my first step into the tech world, where I began developing my skills and passion for technology. I am a self-motivated and determined learner who believes in continuous growth despite challenges.

After joining Freshworks STS Software Academy, I came from a Bio-Maths background with no prior technical knowledge. Initially, it was challenging—even using a laptop was new to me. 2025-2026

Workplace Study Project – Squad Lead

Freshworks STS Software Academy

As part of the Life Skills program, our team was assigned a Workplace Study project to understand how IT companies function. We connected with professionals on LinkedIn and researched seven different IT roles. Each team member was responsible for gathering insights about a specific role. During the project, three members were unable to continue. As the Squad Lead, I reassigned responsibilities among the remaining members, ensuring smooth progress. We successfully completed the research, delivered our presentation, and achieved a top score. My ability to handle the team calmly during a difficult situation was appreciated, and I was recognized as the Best Squad Lead.
